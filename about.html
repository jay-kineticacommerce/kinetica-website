About Kinetica Commerce

Built by an operator, not a pitch deck.

Kinetica Commerce is a Bengaluru-based growth agency built to turn marketplace ad spend into measurable revenue — through retainer-first, dashboard-powered execution rather than slideware.

Jayanth K S

Co-Founder & Head of Client Management

Jayanth brings 10+ years of hands-on e-commerce experience to Kinetica Commerce, with a Digital Marketing with Applied AI certification from IIM Trichy. Before founding Kinetica Commerce, he had impacted more than 500 brands across Amazon India, Flipkart, Walmart, Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart — building the proprietary frameworks that now power every Kinetica Commerce engagement: the PPC Blueprint, the Catalog Dashboard, and the Scenario-Based Optimization Model.

He's also built automated reporting pipelines on Amazon's SP API and Ads API — a rare combination of commercial and technical depth in marketplace services, and the reason Kinetica Commerce leads with dashboards instead of decks.
